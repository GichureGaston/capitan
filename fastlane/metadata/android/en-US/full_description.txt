Capitán is a pocket companion for the sea: conditions, tides, nearby swim spots, and the reef life you meet.

Built for snorkellers, freedivers, surfers, and anyone who plans their weekends around the water.

NOW
See air and "feels like" temperature, wind direction and gusts, humidity, cloud cover, wave height and period, sea-surface temperature, sea level, UV index, visibility, and rain chance for the next few hours. Sunrise and sunset plus a seven-day outlook.

Activity ratings for snorkelling, freediving, surfing, and swimming — Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Dangerous — with the reasons behind each score: waves, wind, gusts, currents, water temperature, and UV. After dark the app suggests rest instead of a water activity.

TIDES AND SEA
A sea-level chart for the next 24 hours, 48 hours, or 7 days; the next high or low tide; upcoming tide turns and the tidal range; wave and current detail; and an hourly wind and gust chart. Modelled tides are guidance, not navigation data.

SPOTS
Find beaches and swimming areas nearby using OpenStreetMap data, see distance, surface type, and patrolled status where available, and rate swimming conditions at each spot. Set a spot as your current location with one tap; Capitán remembers it.

FISH AND CORAL GUIDES
79 fish species and 58 corals and reef-life entries, each with a photo, scientific name, size, diet, habitat, and conservation status. Danger labels are in plain language: harmless, sharp or spiny, stings, venomous, bites when provoked, poisonous to eat. Entries filter automatically to species typical for your waters, with search and a full-guide toggle. All species data and photos are bundled for offline use.

ANYWHERE YOU SWIM
Search for a beach, town, or bay by name, or use your device location. Your chosen location is saved locally.

SAFETY
Capitán is a planning aid, not a lifeguard, a tide table, or a substitute for local knowledge. Conditions are modelled and can be wrong. Tides are estimates. Ratings are heuristics. "Dangerous" means don't. Look, don't touch. Go with a buddy, and go in daylight. If you are ever unsure: don't get in. The sea will still be here tomorrow.

NETWORK AND PERMISSIONS
Weather, marine, tide, search, and nearby-spot data come from Open-Meteo, Nominatim, and the Overpass API, so those features need an internet connection. The fish and coral guides work offline. Location permission is optional: you can search for a place instead. The app contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking. Species photos are from Wikimedia Commons under public-domain, CC0, CC BY, or CC BY-SA licences, with attribution shown in the app.
